# AH Series — ISO A quick release couplings to ISO 7241:2014 Series A
AH Series ISO A couplings are the ideal choice where a tough, durable product of superior quality is required. They are perfectly suited to agricultural and industrial applications.

# Construction
• Carbon steel with trivalent plating (zinc nickel plating available).
• Fitted with Nitrile seals (Viton seals available).
• Also available in AISI 316 stainless steel.
• Also available in Ford style.

# Features
• Optional zinc nickel plating provides corrosion resistance (1200 hours with no red rust in salt spray tests).
• Poppet valves have balanced springs.
• Locking ball system allows quick connection.
• Bidirectional flow.

# Specifications
Standard : ISO 7241:2014 Series A
Sizes : 1/4" to 2" (body size 06 to 50)
Threads : BSPP, NPTF, SAE and metric
Maximum working pressure : 400 bar (5800 psi) for body size 06, down to 170 bar for body size 50
Operating temperature with Nitrile seals : -40°C to 106°C
Operating temperature with Viton seals : -20°C to 200°C

# Applications
• General hydraulics
• Mobile hydraulics
• Construction
• Agricultural

# Performance characteristics (coupled, by body size)
AH06 : max working pressure 400 bar, rated flow 3 l/min
AH10 : max working pressure 350 bar, rated flow 23 l/min
AH12 : max working pressure 320 bar, rated flow 45 l/min
AH19 : max working pressure 300 bar, rated flow 106 l/min
AH25 : max working pressure 250 bar, rated flow 189 l/min
AH32 : max working pressure 250 bar, rated flow 288 l/min

AH50 : max working pressure 170 bar, rated flow 757 l/min
