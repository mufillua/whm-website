# AHF04 flat face coupling — ISO 16028, 350 bar
# Technical data
Standard : ISO 16028 (DN06 to DN30)
Occlusion : Flat face
Locking : Radial balls
Material : Steel
Finishing : Zn-Fe (Cr III) and Zn-Ni
Threads : BSP and NPT
Standard seals : NBR and PU
Optional seals : FKM, EPDM or more
Working temperature : -25°C to +100°C
Working pressure : 350-500 bar
Connection under residual pressure : Not allowed
